      06 — Honesty

      What is known, and what is not

      Exact

      The fold geometry. Every panel in the 3D model is produced by refolding the DXF flat pattern, and the rib-to-spar pilot holes are coordinated between parts.

      Estimated

      Not analysed yet

      Ribs, skin buckling, fittings, landing gear, tail loads, the engine mount and flutter. Mass-balance every control surface.

      Not proven

      No physical part has been cut. Fold one rib and one spar offcut first to check the bend deduction.

      A UK SSDR aircraft needs no airworthiness approval, so all the responsibility is the builder's. Proof-load the wing with sandbags to +4 g and have a BMAA or LAA inspector look at it before it flies. Elsewhere, check your national microlight rules.

      07 — Qualification

      How it gets proven

        An engineering test plan modelled on BCAR Section S practice, not a regulatory approval route. Costs are rough ranges for materials, hire and consumables, not labour, and exclude the airframe itself. The plan lives in qualification.yaml in the repository, and its pass criteria use the live numbers from the build.
